The chart is a line graph that depicts the relationship between follow-up visits and patients meeting the exercise goal.The x-axis repsents follow-up visits from 1 to 4 the y-axis represents patients meeting the exercise goal in percentage. The Home-based program is represented by an orange dashed line with square markers, while the Center-based program is represented by a blue solid line with circular markers. Both programs show an increase in goal attainment from visit 1 to visit 4, with the Home-based program rising from approximately 55% to 70%, and the Center-based program rising from approximately 50% to 75%. At visit 4, there is a 9 percentage-point gap between the two programs. A limitation of this data is that patients chose their program, so the gap does not indicate causation, and each percentage only reflects the patients seen at that visit, which could be influenced by chance or who returned for follow-up.
